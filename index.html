Filings Outreach Scout V10

Targeting and lead intelligence engine analyzing distressed micro-cap filings to isolate actionable recapitalization and restructuring advisory opportunities.

V10 Module #1

Automated Cash Runway & Burn Rate Estimator

Capability: Parses balance sheet cash equivalents and quarterly operating cash flow from 10-Q filings to compute precise months of remaining cash runway.

Rationale: Targeting issuers with under 60 days of cash remaining yields the highest urgency and transaction closing probability for emergency debt and equity line financings.

V10 Module #2

Convertible Debt & Toxic Financing Toxic-Debt Radar

Capability: Scans Item 3.02 unregistered sales and notes payable footnotes to identify variable-rate convertible promissory notes with aggressive floorless ratchet provisions.

Rationale: Companies burdened by death-spiral convertible notes urgently require Chapter 11 recapitalization or Section 3(a)(10) debt exchanges.

V10 Module #3

Algorithmic Advisory Fit & Opportunity Scoring Matrix

Capability: Ranks distressed filers on a 0-100 composite index based on trading float, cash deficit, exchange status, and pending debt maturities.

Rationale: Enables deal origination teams to focus exclusively on top-quintile prospects rather than sifting through hundreds of low-probability filings.

V10 Module #4

Interactive Visual Cap Table Dilution Simulator

Capability: Simulates post-restructuring pro-forma equity dilution under varied recapitalization term sheets directly within the scout UI.

Rationale: Equips advisors with ready-made visual deliverables to present directly to target boards during exploratory pitch meetings.

V10 Module #5

Automated SEC Filing PDF Package Export with Executive Brief

Capability: Generates a branded one-page investment memorandum synthesizing the target's balance sheet distress, debt schedule, and strategic options with one click.

Rationale: Saves 3 hours of manual analyst deck-preparation time per prospect, dramatically scaling advisory pipeline capacity.
